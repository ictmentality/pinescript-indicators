# SSMT (Sequential Smart Money Technique) – Dyad Model

## 1. Foundation: Understanding SMT

Before understanding **SSMT**, you must understand **SMT**.

**SMT (Smart Money Technique)** is a divergence that occurs between two closely correlated assets (a dyad).

In a dyad:

- **Bullish SMT**
  - At least one asset makes a **lower low**
  - The other asset makes a **higher low**
  
- **Bearish SMT**
  - At least one asset makes a **higher high**
  - The other asset makes a **lower high**

This divergence signals internal disagreement between correlated markets and often precedes a directional move.

---

# 2. What is SSMT?

**SSMT (Session/Structured SMT)** builds on SMT by incorporating **Quarterly Theory** and time-based cycle structure.

Instead of looking for random divergences, SSMT requires:

- The divergence to form **between two consecutive quarters**
- The comparison to use the **absolute high or absolute low of the previous quarter**
- Only the **highest high or lowest low of that quarter matters**

Any other highs/lows inside the quarter are irrelevant.

---

# 3. Quarterly Structure (For Dyads)

SSMT must form between **two consecutive quarters**, such as:

- Q1 → Q2  
- Q2 → Q3  
- Q3 → Q4  
- Q4 → Q1  

A valid SSMT requires:

- One asset to take the **previous quarter's absolute high/low**
- The other asset to **fail to take** its own previous quarter’s absolute high/low

### Important Rule
It does **not** matter *when* inside the previous quarter the high/low was formed.  
The only thing that matters is whether it was the **absolute extreme**.

---

# 4. Friday Exception

Within weekly structure:

- Monday (Q1)
- Tuesday (Q2)
- Wednesday (Q3)
- Thursday (Q4)

Friday does not technically fall under a quarter, but it can still be treated as one.

You can look for SSMT between:
- Thursday → Friday
- Friday → Monday

---

# 5. What SSMT Indicates

SSMT is used to identify:

- **Manipulation**
- **Reversals**
- Distribution following manipulation

After manipulation, we typically expect **distribution**, meaning directional continuation.

SSMT can often provide direction for the next **1–3 quarters**.

---

# 6. Example Logic (Dyad)

Imagine two correlated assets:

### Scenario: Bullish SSMT

- Asset A takes the previous quarter’s **absolute low**
- Asset B fails to take its previous quarter’s low

This suggests:
- Asset A performed a liquidity sweep
- Smart money divergence is present
- Upside distribution may follow

---

### Scenario: Bearish SSMT

- Asset A takes the previous quarter’s **absolute high**
- Asset B fails to take its previous quarter’s high

This suggests:
- Liquidity grab above prior highs
- Bearish divergence
- Downside distribution may follow

---

# 7. True Opens (Time-Based Filters)

SSMT must be filtered using **True Opens (TO)**.

The first candle of Q2 in every cycle defines the True Open.

### True Opens by Cycle

- **True Year Open (TYO)** – First Monday of April  
- **True Month Open (TMO)** – Second Monday of the month  
- **True Week Open (TWO)** – Second day of the week at 6:00PM (Monday 18:00)  
- **True Day Open (TDO)** – Every day at 00:00  
- **True Session Open (TSO)**  
  - Asia – 19:30  
  - London – 01:30  
  - New York AM – 07:30  
  - New York PM – 13:30  

### Directional Filter Rule

- **Bullish SSMT must occur below the True Open**
- **Bearish SSMT must occur above the True Open**

If this condition is not met, the setup is lower probability.

---

# 8. Filtering High-Probability SSMTs (Dyads)

Many traders attempt to trade every divergence. This is a mistake.

Use the following filters:

---

## 1. Zoom Out (Higher Time Frame Bias)

- If HTF structure is bullish → focus only on bullish SSMTs
- Counter-trend SSMTs tend to fail or become shallow retracements

---

## 2. High-Impact News (Red Folder Events)

If SSMT forms via a high-impact news event:
- Probability increases significantly
- News-driven manipulation often fuels liquidity sweeps

---

## 3. True Open Positioning

If you see:

- Bullish SSMT forming **above** the True Open → low probability
- Bearish SSMT forming **below** the True Open → low probability

Location relative to the True Open is critical.

---

# 9. Summary (Dyad Model)

SSMT is a structured SMT divergence between two correlated assets that:

1. Forms between consecutive quarters  
2. Uses the previous quarter’s absolute high/low  
3. Signals manipulation and likely distribution  
4. Is filtered using True Opens  
5. Aligns with higher time frame bias  

When properly filtered, SSMT can provide directional bias for the next **1–3 quarters**.

It is not about trading every divergence.  
It is about identifying structured, time-aligned manipulation between two correlated assets and positioning for distribution.
